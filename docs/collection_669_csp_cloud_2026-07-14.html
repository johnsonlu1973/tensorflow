Amazon SQS turns 20: Two decades of reliable messaging at scale
Amazon SQS 邁向 20 週年：二十年來大規模可靠訊息傳遞
On July 13, 2006, we launched Amazon Simple Queue Service (Amazon SQS) as one of the first three services available to customers, alongside Amazon EC2 and Amazon S3 . We had learned firsthand that distributed systems need a reliable way to pass messages between components without creating tight dependencies. If one service called another directly and that service was slow or unavailable, failures cascaded through the entire system. Message queuing solved this by letting services communicate asynchronously: a producer could drop a message into a queue and move on, while a consumer picked it up when ready. This approach kept individual service failures from affecting the rest of the system. When Amazon SQS launched publicly in July 2006, it made this pattern available to every AWS customer.
Amazon SQS 於 2006 年 7 月 13 日推出，成為首批三項可用服務之一，與 Amazon EC2 和 Amazon S3 並列。該服務為分散式系統提供了可靠的元件間訊息傳遞方式，無需建立緊密耦合。
