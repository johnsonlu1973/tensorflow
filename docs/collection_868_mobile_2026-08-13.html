Google launches Tap to Share for Pixels and Samsung's latest devices
For a while now, Google has been rumored to be working on an Android equivalent of Apple's NameDrop. Today, this has become official as Tap to Share. It is available on Pixels from the Pixel 6 onwards, and Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra, and Flip8. Google promises to expand support for the feature to more devices "by the end of the year". As you'd expect from the name, this works by tapping two supported devices together. When that happens, stuff is shared - contact info, photos, videos, "and more". It's really that simple.
