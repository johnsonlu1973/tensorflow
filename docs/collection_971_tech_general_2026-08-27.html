Bentley provided flights from Washington, DC, to Monterey, California, and accommodation so Ars could ride in the Torcal, as well as some other things you can read about in the coming weeks. The addition of electric motors perfectly suits the marque : what's more Bentley than waves of effortless torque, after all? But until now, there's always been a combustion engine along for the ride.
