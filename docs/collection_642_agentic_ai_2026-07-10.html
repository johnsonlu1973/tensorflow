What they found ranges from the mundane to the unnerving. Researchers at the company built a tool called the Jacobian lens (or J-lens) and used it to uncover a hidden area, which they named the J-space, inside Claude Opus 4.6, a version of Anthropic’s flagship LLM released in February. The J-space contains individual words that are related to the words and phrases that the model is most likely to spit out in a response in the near future.
