Taking to X late on Monday, SpaceXAI announced the new pricing structure. It has five tiers, from free up to $200 per month, with the cheapest paid option costing $8. Paid tiers share a single pool of Grok-model usage across eligible products, and existing Cursor subscribers can keep their current plans &#8212; for now, at least. However, arguably the bigger news for developers is the change to how Cursor charges for AI models. While the new subscriptions offer more included coding usage with Grok, customers who prefer competing models from the likes of Anthropic and OpenAI will face additional charges.
