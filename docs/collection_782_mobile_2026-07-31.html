The Redmi K100 Pro continues to leak ahead of its launch. A new poster making rounds on Weibo gives us our best look at the device yet. The overall design looks similar to the Redmi K90 Pro Max with a triple camera setup inside an island configuration. We can also spot what appears to be a subwoofer, which could again feature Bose branding and sound tuning. The more interesting bit is the color choice. The dark red is quite similar to the rumored Cherry-red color that’s expected to be the hero hue for the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max. Funnily enough, Xiaomi will beat Apple to the punch...
Redmi K100 Pro 在推出前繼續洩露，新海報提供了迄今為止最清晰的設備外觀，設計與 Redmi K90 Pro Max 類似。
